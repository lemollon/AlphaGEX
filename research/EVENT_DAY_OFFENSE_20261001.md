# Event-day offense: predeclared research extension

Authorized October 1, 2026: test strategies that can earn money on days when Spark/Flame struggle. This is a queued extension, not a completed or profitable backtest. Finish and independently audit the running 6c7630 dynamic protection replay before replacing its numerical source.

Primary objective: lower combined-account drawdowns, positive profit after fees/subscriptions in natural and adverse fills, and profitable chronological final-year validation. Fewer trades and lower profit than baseline are acceptable. Report worst daily dollar/percentage losses as well as closed drawdown; neither is a guaranteed loss ceiling. Original more-trades/more-profit goal remains a secondary comparison.

Test three separate mechanisms on SPY first:

1. Downside breakout debit spread: after two completed closes below the preceding 15-minute low, with a completed five-minute SPY decline of at least 0.25% and VIX rise of at least 3%, buy an ATM put and sell a put $2 below. Compare a 5% VIX-rise confirmation. Mirror a call spread for an upside breakout, requiring SPY five-minute rise of at least 0.25% and no VIX rise. Enter at the next valid synchronized quote, never at the signal bar's low/high. Use nearest available strike determined from contemporaneous spot, not future price or hindsight deltas.
2. Post-release continuation: FOMC entries only after a completed 14:05 or 14:15 ET bar; CPI/payroll entries only after completed 09:35 or 09:45 ET bars because those releases precede market open. Break the preceding five-minute completed range, require two confirming closes, and buy the corresponding $2 ATM debit spread. Treat the 14:30 Fed press conference as another risk window, not a known direction. Compare closing before that window versus holding with the same exit controller.
3. Protective overlay: when a threatened put-credit host produces the same causal downside signal, compare closing it, buying one defined-risk put debit spread, and closing then opening that spread. Do not automatically reverse solely because a loss occurred. Model combined cash and margin at the action time; report standalone hedge cost, combined net profit, and losses on false alarms. Do not count an avoided host loss as hedge revenue.

For each family, compare fixed daily debit budgets of 0.5% and 1% of day-start equity. Skip when one spread exceeds the budget; never force a minimum lot that breaches it. No scaling up after losses. Maximum one offensive entry per day; no fresh entries after 15:30 ET. Compare 50% versus 100% debit profit targets and 50% debit stop signals, using subsequent executable quotes; all unresolved triggered closes block/rollback the session. Exit by the existing session guard. Target/stop prices are signals, not guaranteed fills.

Compare scheduled FOMC-only, scheduled major-event days, and all-day signal triggers to detect whether event labels add value. Evaluate all eligible dates, not only known baseline loss dates. Preserve event-day no-trade and protection-only controls. Train through 2025-09-26; select using training only, then inspect 2025-09-29 onward without reselecting. Historical validation is retrospective, not untouched. Report event counts and uncertainty; small event samples cannot establish reliable daily recovery.

Use fresh historical multi-leg bid/ask, correct sides and noncrossed quotes, displayed size checks, fees per contract/side, natural/adverse execution and complete cash accounting. Confirm fee assumptions for bought options rather than blindly reusing a credit-spread fee. No hypothetical surface prices as realized profits, no same-bar lookahead, no stale performance inheritance. New numerical source requires a new source hash and full replay with independent ledger audit. Missing quotes must be distinguished from transient provider failures. No live changes or broker/order endpoints.

A pre-event straddle is a separate benchmark only if the data and cost budget support it: both purchased premiums must be overcome. A large market move alone does not prove a profitable options trade. Do not promote an untested straddle or claim it recovers losses.
